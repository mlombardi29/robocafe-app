### Scale calibration check

`scale_check` · Every visit · required

1. Set your separate (independent) scale to 0 g. Fill a cup with water until it weighs 200 g on that scale.
2. At the kiosk, make sure nothing is on the scale.
3. Open the Scale API and press Reset the scale to 0.
4. Put the 200 g cup of water on the scale.
5. Press Get Weight.
6. If it reads between 190 g and 210 g inclusive, the scale is fine.
7. If not, press Calibrate to 200g.
8. Press Get Weight. It should read 200 g.
9. If it still will not read 200 g, post in the WhatsApp channel.
**Additional info**
- Do this every visit, before maintenance mode when practical. Maintenance Mode is its own checklist step. This is the UofT drink/ice scale — not the Sinai/RBC robot arm Auto Calibrate weight check.

## 3. Milk cleaning

### Start milk module cleaning

`start_milk_clean` · Every visit · required · Not needed blocked on new visits after 0016

1. Press Clean on the milk frother and follow the prompts.
2. Fill the water pitcher with 750 mL of water and 30 mL of blue Rinza.
3. When the frother tells you to, take the tubes out of the milk and put them in the water.
4. Keep the fridge door shut.
**Additional info**
- The cleaning runs about 9 minutes. Don't touch the module while it runs. UofT uses Rinza here — not EverClean.

## 4. While the cleaning runs

### Change the milk bag if it is due

`milk_bag` · Every 4 days · required

No milk stays in a bag more than 4 days. Don't touch the tubes while they are in the cleaning pitcher. When you put the tubes back in the milk, the suction tube must sit 1 inch lower than the sensor tube.
**Additional info**
- UofT has one milk, 2%. An uncovered pitcher or an open fridge door during cleaning lets steam freeze inside the fridge.

### Top up 2% milk

`milk_topup` · Every visit · required

This kiosk uses 2% only. Don't touch the tubes.

### Coffee concentrate

Minimum two-thirds full. If below two-thirds, refill. If at or above two-thirds, leave alone.

### Syrup cleaning and top-ups

`syrup` · Every visit · required · Not needed blocked on new visits after 0016

1. Open the syrup drawer.
2. Press Clean.
3. Follow the prompts on the syrup containers.
4. At the final tube refill, when syrup is visible from all spouts, press OK so it does not keep pouring.
5. Wipe the bottles with hot water.
6. Push the nozzles at the end of the tubes fully down so they reach deep into the liquid, not just the surface.
7. Caps tight on top.
8. Run a pipe cleaner through the syrup nozzles so they don't clog.
**Additional info**
- At UofT (2.2), move the cup holder out of the way. At Sinai (3.5) and RBC (S5), park the robotic arm out of the way.

### Clean the powder nozzles

`powder_clean` · Every visit · required · Not needed blocked on new visits after 0016

1. Lay a damp paper towel under the dispenser first to catch fallout.
2. Turn the nozzles upward and take out the funnels.
3. Check for powder buildup or residue. If there is residue, deep clean it, and it must be completely dry before you put it back. Wetness causes more buildup.
4. Vacuum any loose powder with the handheld vacuum.
5. Turn all three powder spouts back to down-facing, or powder drinks come out as plain hot water.
**Additional info**
- Powdered matcha is not in use right now — do not spend time servicing an unused matcha container. Do not delete history for past matcha rows.
