## 4. While the cleaning runs

### Change the milk bag if it is due

`milk_bag` · Every 4 days · required

No milk stays in a bag more than 4 days. Don't touch the tubes. During cleaning the tubes are in the hot water and Everclean container, so you can change the bag and top up milk. The milk suction tube sits 2 cm above the bottom of the milk bag. The milk sensor tube (a plastic tube running alongside the suction tube that tells the module whether there's still milk) sits 2 cm above the bottom of the suction tube, about 4 cm above the bag bottom. Neither should touch the bottom.

### Syrup cleaning and top-ups

`syrup` · Every visit · required · Not needed blocked on new visits after 0015

1. Open the syrup drawer.
2. Press Clean.
3. Follow the prompts on the syrup containers.
4. At the final tube refill, when syrup is visible from all spouts, press OK so it does not keep pouring.
5. Wipe the bottles with hot water.
6. Push the nozzles at the end of the tubes fully down so they reach deep into the liquid, not just the surface.
7. Caps tight on top.
8. Run a pipe cleaner through the syrup nozzles so they don't clog.
**Additional info**
- At UofT (2.2), move the cup holder out of the way. At Sinai (3.5) and RBC (S5), park the robotic arm out of the way.

### Clean the powder nozzles

`powder_clean` · Every visit · required · Not needed blocked on new visits after 0015

1. Lay a damp paper towel under the dispenser first to catch fallout.
2. Turn the nozzles upward and take out the funnels.
3. Check for powder buildup or residue. If there is residue, deep clean it, and it must be completely dry before you put it back. Wetness causes more buildup.
4. Vacuum any loose powder with the handheld vacuum.
5. Turn all three powder spouts back to down-facing, or powder drinks come out as plain hot water.
