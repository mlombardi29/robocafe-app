## Read this first

- Follow the section order below. Do not invent a new required sequence.
- **Robot arm calibration** (Sinai and RBC only) uses the seven-step Auto Calibrate weight check (**190–210 g inclusive**). That is **not** the cup presence / arm sensor check.
- **Arm sensor check**: Mount Sinai uses solid-green hot/cold criteria. At RBC mark Not needed (Sinai-only).
- After migration `0016`, new visits disallow **Not needed** on the mandatory keys listed at the bottom. Older and offline snapshots without that flag stay finishable as they were.
- Beans refill only below two-thirds. Powder stays below half. Kiosoft milk entry: actual added minus 500 mL (manual entry only; not a KioskOps inventory change).
- No lock-box codes, fridge codes, key-card PINs, or private catalogue data belong in these instructions.

### Robot arm calibration

`arm_calibration` · Weekly · required · Auto Calibrate weight procedure

1. On the order tablet go to Robot API.
2. Press Get Weight. It should read 0 g.
3. Press Close Claw and place the weighing cup from the top into the claw.
4. Press Get Weight. 190–210 g inclusive is successful.
5. Remove the weighing cup. Open the claw using the button.
6. If the weight is outside that range, still remove the cup and open the claw, then press Auto Calibrate. The robot picks up the cup itself.
7. When it does, read the displayed weight. It should say 200 and success.
**Additional info**
- Do not press a manual Calibrate 200 command. This weight check is not the cup presence sensor.

### Arm sensor check

`arm_sensor` · Weekly · required · Sinai-only — Not needed at RBC

This arm sensor solid-green check is for Mount Sinai only. At RBC, mark Not needed.
**Additional info**

`long_water` · Every visit · required · Not needed blocked on new visits after 0016

## 3. Milk cleaning

### Start milk cleaning

`start_milk_clean` · Every visit · required · Not needed blocked on new visits after 0016

1. Cameo menu, top middle, Clean.
2. Follow the prompts on the screen.
3. Use a container with 30 mL of EverClean (not Rinza) when the Cameo asks for cleaner.
**Additional info**
- The cleaning runs about 18 minutes. EverClean is required here — do not use blue Rinza for this Cameo milk clean.

## 4. While the cleaning runs

### Change the milk bag if it is due

`milk_bag` · Every 4 days · required

No milk stays in a bag more than 4 days. Count days since the fresh bag, not shows. Don't touch the tubes. During cleaning the tubes are in the hot water and EverClean container (30 mL EverClean, not Rinza), so you can change the bag.

### Top up 2% and oat

`milk_topup` · Every visit · required

Don't touch the tubes.

### Grey water

`grey_water` · Every visit · required

RBC / PRACTICE RBC only. Grey water collects in a container underneath the kiosk with a coiled hose (not a fixed direct drain into plumbing). 1. Uncoil the hose and position the end near the ground drain. 2. Turn the handle/clasp so the container drains through the hose into that drain. 3. Turn the clasp back to perpendicular (closed) BEFORE you bring the hose in, and coil the hose back up.
**Additional info**
- If the clasp stays open, the next drink's grey water floods inside the kiosk.

### Syrup cleaning and top-ups

`syrup` · Every visit · required · Not needed blocked on new visits after 0016

1. Open the syrup drawer.
2. Press Clean.
3. Follow the prompts on the syrup containers.
4. At the final tube refill, when syrup is visible from all spouts, press OK so it does not keep pouring.
5. Wipe the bottles with hot water.
6. Push the nozzles at the end of the tubes fully down so they reach deep into the liquid, not just the surface.
7. Caps tight on top.
8. Run a pipe cleaner through the syrup nozzles so they don't clog.
**Additional info**
- At UofT (2.2), move the cup holder out of the way. At Sinai (3.5) and RBC (S5), park the robotic arm out of the way.

### Clean the powder nozzles

`powder_clean` · Every visit · required · Not needed blocked on new visits after 0016

1. Lay a damp paper towel under the dispenser first to catch fallout.
2. Turn the nozzles upward and take out the funnels.
3. Check for powder buildup or residue. If there is residue, deep clean it, and it must be completely dry before you put it back. Wetness causes more buildup.
4. Vacuum any loose powder with the handheld vacuum.
5. Turn all three powder spouts back to down-facing, or powder drinks come out as plain hot water.
**Additional info**
- Powdered matcha is not in use right now — do not spend time servicing an unused matcha container. Do not delete history for past matcha rows.

### Powders

`powder_fill` · When below the fill line · required

Fill only if below half. Matcha is unused — leave that container alone.

### Cups and lids

`cups_lids` · When below the fill line · required

Fill cups and lids to the top. Gently twist stuck 16 oz cold cups apart before loading, then seat them lightly in the cup holder. Otherwise they jam at the top and won't dispense.

### Cup and drink garbage

`garbage` · Check and decide · required

Quick look. Empty it only if it is worth it.

### Clean Robot Claw (delicately)

`claw` · Check and decide · required

1. The arm and claw are very sensitive to pressure and easy to knock off calibration.
2. Use very hot water on a towel and press lightly.
3. Don't scrub and don't push.

## 6. Close up

### Close-up check

`door_check` · Every visit · required · Not needed blocked on new visits after 0016

1. The drink dispensing elevators sit higher when the kiosk is running than when they are open for servicing.
2. They slide down to the customer opening, and there is less space at the top.
3. Leave clear space above anything you put back, and do not stack anything into that path.
4. A blocked elevator takes the kiosk out of service and can break it.
**Additional info**
- Other doors on the kiosk are separate. Do not block those either.

### Update consumables in KC Manager

`kc_consumables` · Every visit · required

Enter what you filled. Confirm updates in command history.
**Additional info**
- For milk in the Kiosoft consumables app only: enter the actual amount added minus 500 mL (example: add 8,000 mL → enter 7,500 mL). This does not change how much milk you actually add, and it does not change KioskOps inventory or rewrite history. Do not use the old 1,000–1,500 mL under-report text.
